SpeakEasy Mini - Third-Party Notices
=====================================

Artifact status
---------------
This notice accompanies an unsigned Windows artifact, built for personal
local use rather than public distribution. The exact Rust and npm
dependency versions are recorded in Cargo.lock and package-lock.json.

Bundled native runtime
----------------------
No third-party native runtime is bundled. This section declared sherpa-onnx,
ONNX Runtime and the NVIDIA CUDA redistributables until 2026-08-18; all three
left with the streaming engine, and the installer payload is now three
executables with no native library beside them. llama.cpp is compiled directly
into granite-worker.exe and is covered below.

The CUDA redistributables are still fetched on demand, by machines that
qualify, and verified by digest against models/trusted-manifest.json. They are
not redistributed in this artifact.
