Nemotron 3.5 Streaming model material
--------------------------------------
The GPU streaming pack is a SpeakEasy-derived float32 ONNX export of
NVIDIA's nemotron-3.5-asr-streaming-0.6b checkpoint. It is hosted separately
from the Windows artifact at:
https://huggingface.co/orangeblue39/nemotron-3.5-streaming-en-cuda

Copyright NVIDIA Corporation
License: OpenMDW-1.1
Modification: re-exported to float32 ONNX by SpeakEasy's pinned
scripts/model-export environment; this is not an NVIDIA release archive.
The complete OpenMDW-1.1 agreement is retained in
packaging/licenses/OpenMDW-1.1.txt and in the Hugging Face model repository.
The app verifies the hosted archive length and SHA-256 before activation.
